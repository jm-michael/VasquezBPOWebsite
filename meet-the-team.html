AUDIT

Our team is comprised of BS Accountancy graduates, the majority of whom have acquired CPA certification. With our excellent educational background and extensive training, rest assured that your audits are in the hands of experts.

We pride ourselves on being well-versed in handling audits across diverse industries. From Government to EBP, Healthcare to Not- for-Profit, and even SEC/Private engagements, we've got you covered. Our team's breadth of experience ensures that we understand the unique challenges and intricacies of each sector.

ACCOUNTING

Our team is a dynamic group of highly skilled business graduates who are passionate about numbers and finance.

From general accounting to month-end closing, billing and collections, invoice processing, and financial statement preparation, our experts are well-versed in all aspects of the accounting process. No task is too big or too small for us.

With our attention to detail and commitment to accuracy, you can trust us to handle all your accounting tasks efficiently and effectively.

TAX

Our Tax Team is composed of Accountancy graduates who have a proficient understanding of taxation. Whether it's personal tax returns or business tax returns, our team is able to handle a wide range of tax-related matters for various types of businesses including partnerships, corporations, trusts, and more.

With a deep knowledge of different tax applications and software, our Tax Team ensures accurate and efficient preparation of regulatory and compliance forms. We understand that tax filings can be overwhelming, but rest assured, our team is here to make the process as smooth as possible.

BUSINESS OPERATIONS

Our Business Operations Team, made up of dedicated professionals, is the driving force behind seamless processes in Human Resources, Finance, IT, Marketing, and Payroll.

They are the backbone of our organization, working diligently behind the scenes to support our employees and streamline our business functions.

At the heart of our operations, our Business Operations Team keeps everything in sync, so our employees can focus on what they do best.
