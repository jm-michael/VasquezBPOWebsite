Breaking Barriers with Vasquez: A Blueprint for Excellence

April 5-7, 2024

The Park Silang

Our recent company event, held at The Park Silang from April 5 to 7, was a landmark gathering that blended professional development and team spirit. The event commenced with a Town Hall on April 5, where the theme "Breaking Barriers with Vasquez: A Blueprint for Excellence" set the stage for transformative discussions. Our esteemed speakers, including associate managers, managers, directors, and partners, delivered inspiring talks on overcoming organizational challenges to achieve collective success. They presented solutions set to be rolled out across the organization, marking a new chapter of growth and collaboration.

A significant highlight of the Town Hall was the recognition of our loyalty awardees, Caitlin and Lorielyn, who celebrated their 5th year with the firm. Both joined Vasquez Advantage as Senior Associates in Audit and have since advanced to Manager positions, exemplifying their dedication and growth within the company.

Testimonies from Emart, Kim, Nice, and Mark Lloyd enriched the event with personal stories of growth and achievement within the company.

The excitement continued April 6 and 7 with our first-ever firmwide face-to-face team- building event. Employees from the Audit, Accounting, Tax, and HR/Admin departments were grouped into five mixed teams, engaging in a variety of physical and mental challenges. These activities, designed to be both fun and demanding, took place on land and water, encouraging teamwork and innovation.

The evening's "Sundown Tropics" socials night further enhanced the experience, as participants showcased their summer spirit with bright and colorful attire.

The event concluded with the announcement of "Cris-team" as the team-building champions. Their victory underscored the power of collaboration and the importance of breaking down silos within the organization. This event was a significant step towards building a more cohesive and successful future for our organization.
